🌸 Your Perfect Holiday Day in Aachen

Whit Monday • 25 May 2026

Family Edition • Starting from Promenadenstraße 44

A magical day full of history, relaxation & golden memories 💛

📍 Your Home Base

Promenadenstraße 44, 52062 Aachen

Everything is a beautiful short walk away — no stress, no car needed today!

Start your day right here in the heart of the old town 🌳

☀️ Morning • 9:00 – 11:00

Elisenbrunnen

The iconic neoclassical thermal springs pavilion — Aachen’s most photographed landmark.

Enjoy a coffee at a nearby café, snap lovely photos, and pop into the Tourist Information for free maps & insider tips.

⛪ Midday • 13:00 – 18:00

Aachen Cathedral

Special holiday hours today! • Free entry • Last entry 17:45

Marvel at Charlemagne’s throne in the Palatine Chapel and the stunning golden mosaics. A true UNESCO wonder!

👗 Tip: Modest clothing recommended inside the Cathedral. Arrive a little early — it’s a public holiday but still magical with fewer crowds than weekends.

🧖 Afternoon • 15:30 – 19:30

Carolus Thermen

4 glorious hours of thermal relaxation in one of Germany’s most beautiful spa complexes

Indoor & outdoor pools, saunas, steam rooms, quiet zones… the perfect way to unwind after your morning of sightseeing. Open until 23:00!

Don’t forget: swimsuit + flip-flops (towels & bathrobes available to rent on site)

🌆 Evening • 19:30 onwards

Old Town Stroll & Dinner

Wander the historic Market Square, Town Hall & Domhof as the sun sets

Choose a cosy restaurant for a delicious holiday dinner. The illuminated Cathedral glowing against the evening sky is pure magic ✨

✅ Today’s Practical Tips (Whit Monday Public Holiday)

🚗 Street parking is completely FREE all day (exactly like Sundays & public holidays!)

🛍️ Shops mostly closed — but every attraction & most restaurants are open and ready for you

🌤️ Beautiful spring weather ~18–22°C — ideal for walking the old town

🏊 Swimsuit + flip-flops essential for Carolus Thermen

👟 Comfortable walking shoes (you’ll do ~8,000 steps of pure joy!)

📱 Download the free “Aachen” app or use Google Maps for easy navigation

🎉 Fun Facts About Aachen

  • 🏰 Charlemagne chose Aachen as his favourite city — the Cathedral was built especially for him in 800 AD
  • ♨️ The healing thermal springs have been enjoyed since Roman times — over 2,000 years of relaxation!
  • 👑 More than 30 German kings and queens were crowned right here in Aachen Cathedral
  • 🌍 It is the oldest cathedral north of the Alps
  • 🍪 Don’t leave without trying the famous Aachener Printen — the city’s special spiced gingerbread!
